Last week I gave a ‘tech talk’ talk on GeoServer at Google, which was a fun experience. It’s for employees of Google, but they make the talk available to all on Google Video: [GeoServer and Architectures of Participation for Geospatial Information](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=-3923618086187138447). If you like it give a nice rating on the page. It touches on The Open Planning Project (my employer), how we leveraged standards to implement KML output in GeoServer to talk to Google Earth, and goes a bit in to architectures of participation for geodata, which I’m in the midst of writing about in this blog.

It’s 54 minutes long, perhaps in the future I’ll link to specific chunks of it. I’m also making the slides available ([creative commons attribution](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) license) in [powerpoint](http://docs.codehaus.org/download/attachments/61286/geoserver-tech-talk-ch.ppt) and [open document](http://docs.codehaus.org/download/attachments/61286/geoserver-tech-talk-ch.odp).
