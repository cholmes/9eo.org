The power of AI has enabled me to complete a project I've long wanted to, but could never muster the energy to pull off, which is to gather my public writing all in one place. I started blogging at [cholmes.wordpress.com](https://cholmes.wordpress.com), and then for reasons that escape me right now (nicer writing interface? more potential readers?) I switched to [cholmes.medium.com](https://cholmes.medium.com). I was pretty happy with Medium for awhile, but it seems to be entering the second stage of [enshittification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enshittification). The experience may be good for paying users and writers seeking to monetize their content, but all I've ever wanted is a nice place to write that's easy for everyone to read.

I'd been sitting on the domain 9eo.org for awhile, just to have an independent professional-ish email address that wasn't a gmail. But a couple months ago I realized that it'd be trivial to get a decent website out of claude, and to have it gather all my posts in one place. I managed to also include some that I wrote for other blogs, like OGC's blog, that I never had in my feed. It's cool to see most everything I've written publicly in one place:

![Scrolling through the full archive of posts on 9eo.org](/assets/img/posts/a-writing-reboot/9eo-posts.gif)

Check it out at [9eo.org/blog](/blog/). And this is the first post that will be first published on the new blog. I'll continue to cross-post at [cholmes.medium.com](https://cholmes.medium.com), since there still seems to be quite a few people who read it that way, I'll also post on linkedin when I write new ones, and will continue to post on [cloudnativegeo.org](https://cloudnativegeo.org) for those topics.

It's interesting to me to see which years I wrote a lot (2006 & 2018), and which ones I didn't manage to write anything (2009-2012, 2014-2017). This year is looking pretty dire so far, with only two posts, which are basically the same thing - announcing geoparquet-io. I'm hoping to reverse the trend, though I've now done this long enough to know that it's not a good idea to make any promises about how often I am going to manage to post.

But I do have an 'excuse' to why I've written less, and I do see a shift coming that may open up writing more. The excuse is that I've been too busy actually building stuff, and AI coding tools have flipped the equation for me, where it now often makes sense for me to put my time into building. My main goal for at least a decade has been to improve the geospatial ecosystem by focusing on foundational formats and infrastructure to enable more accessible geospatial information. With STAC and GeoParquet I'd lead the spec work, but it made more sense for me to focus on evangelism and community building and pair with great developers who would build out the tools to make the ecosystems real. For my next major spec + ecosystem it's made more sense to use claude and work with a smaller number of collaborators to get the foundations in place. But we're about to start talking publicly and really trying to grow the community next week.

So I suspect it's going to make sense for me to deprioritize building and dust off my writing skills to start to drive adoption. I'm also hoping to write more about geoparquet-io, as we've built quite a bit of cool functionality there and I'd like to highlight it more, and it serves as a core foundation for this next big project. I had thought I might try to leverage AI to help with writing, but I think I'm going to keep this blog AI free except for the lightest of touches to edit my worst grammar and language quirks (ie when I use the same word 5 times in two paragraphs instead of finding good synonyms).

So add [9eo.org's feed](/feed.xml) to your RSS reader, if anyone still uses those. Or just watch for new posts on linkedin or on medium, and maybe someday I'll see about getting back on blue sky. Hope everyone had a great summer!
