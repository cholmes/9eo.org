## OGC API — Features & STAC Happy Hour in Alexandria, VA this tuesday!

Want to learn more about the new [OGC API — Features](https://github.com/opengeospatial/ogcapi-features) 1.0 standard (formerly known as [WFS3](https://medium.com/@cholmes/wfs-3-0-get-excited-yes-8e904fdbcc0))? Or have you heard people talk about [STAC](http://stacspec.org) (SpatioTemporal Asset Catalogs) but haven’t been quite sure what it is? If you live in the Washington DC area please [join us](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/stac-happy-hour-element-84-registration-78510696607) at [Element 84](https://www.element84.com/)’s office in Alexandria on Tuesday, November 6th at 6:00 for drinks, snacks, lightning talks and friendly conversation.

We’re doing the event in conjunction with the [sprint at CosmiQ Works](https://medium.com/radiant-earth-insights/join-stac-sprint-5-ogc-api-hackathon-november-5-7-48178137f778), as we’re going to have so many members of both communities in town. We figured it’d be good to do a little outreach and share what’s going on with these two great specifications. There will be lightning talks from Azavea, Planet, Maxar, USGS, Element 84, Climate Corp and more, talking about their work.
